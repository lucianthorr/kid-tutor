Says

Trace / copy: “Write the word cat.”

Look, cover, write: “Look at this word: cat.” … (hides) “Now write cat.”

Done: “Yes! C, A, T. Cat!”

Behavior

  • How Writing differs from Spelling: here the word is shown, because the goal is neat letters, not remembering the spelling. Level 3 (“look, cover, write”, a common school routine) is the bridge to Spelling.
  • Levels 1–2: one box per letter, checked like Spelling: red and clear on a miss, faded letter after a second miss.
  • Level 3: one wide box for the whole word. The app splits the ink into letters where strokes don’t overlap and checks each with the child’s recognizer; if that fails, Apple’s on-device text reader gets a second look. A miss clears the box; a second miss shows the word faded to trace.
  • Levels and mastery work like letters: clean pass up, needed a trace down, two clean passes at level 3 mastered.
  • Words join a session once about 8 letters reach level 3, and only words whose letters have all been practiced. Then a session is roughly 3 letters and 3 words.
  • Word list: the same starter words as Spelling (cat, dog, sun, … because), shortest first.

Open questions

  • Does the wide box check well enough with real Pencil writing? Letters written touching each other can’t be split.
