Which one doesn't belong?

← What comes next?Next: Finish the grid →

Says

On appear: “Three of these go together. Which one doesn't belong?”

Tap a tile: that's the answer. There is no Check button: every puzzle has one answer, so the right or wrong reply comes straight away, and it names the tile.

Right: “Yes! The others are all circles. The triangle doesn't belong.”

Wrong: “Hmm, the blue circle is a circle like the red one. Try another.” and it fades.

Help: “Look at the shapes.” (it names what to compare, not the answer.) Again: “Three of them are circles.”

Behavior

  • Four big tiles; tapping one answers (no Check button).
  • Exactly one answer, checked by tests: the other three share something the odd tile doesn't (here, being a circle). Every other feature is either the same on all four tiles or different on all four (here, color), so no second answer can sneak in.
  • Levels: one obvious difference with everything else the same (three red circles and a red square) → shown here: the other things vary too → size as the difference (big vs small, all one shape: four stars with one small, since a small square among a big circle, triangle and star just looks like another different tile) → any of the three.
  • A wrong pick shows why: the three that go together light up in blue for two seconds, as the app says what they share.
  • Each Help press says a hint and takes away a wrong choice.
