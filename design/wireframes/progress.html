Behavior

  • A Progress section at the top of Settings lists each subject with a bar and its counts. Tapping one opens that subject's page.
  • The deck and its three piles, like Anki: to do = never practiced (Anki's "new"), in progress = practiced but not learned yet ("learning"), learned = mastered ("mature").
  • Learned means: Reading and Math, three first-try answers more than hints (box 3 of 5); Spelling and Writing, two first tries in a row at the top level (whole word from memory, letter from memory). A hint takes "learned" away again.
  • Subject page: four numbers, the bar, then a picker for the three piles. In progress opens first, hardest first (lowest level, most tries), with what's often missed. To do is in teaching order, so it shows what comes next.
  • Decks: Reading 150 cards (words and sentences); Spelling 123 words; Writing 185: the 62 characters and the 123 spelling words, which join once 8 lowercase letters are written from memory (shown in groups: lowercase, numbers, capitals, words); Math every fact, grouped by skill (adding to 5, … taking away within 20).
  • Puzzles have no deck (each puzzle is made fresh), so their page shows each kind's level, puzzles solved and the current streak instead.
  • Reset: each subject page ends with "Reset Spelling progress"; the Progress section ends with "Reset all progress". Both ask first, keep tokens and won toys, and copy the old file to Progress › Backups.
  • Grown-up tabs: Settings, Test, Results, Calibrate. The portal opens on Settings, or on Calibrate while handwriting still needs samples.
  • Normal iPad list UI, since this is a grown-up screen inside Settings.
