What comes next?

← HomeNext: Odd one out →

Says

On appear: “What comes next? Red circle, blue square, red circle, blue square, red circle… Tap the one that comes next.”

Tap a choice: that's the answer. There is no Check button: every puzzle has one answer, so the right or wrong reply comes straight away, and it names the tile.

Right: “Yes! Red, blue, red, blue, red… blue!” (the rule, said back.)

Wrong: “Not quite. Listen: red, blue, red, blue, red…” and that choice fades.

Help: “Look at the ones that are the same. What comes after a red circle?”

Behavior

  • Tapping a choice answers: right fills the yellow gap, wrong fades the choice. No Check button.
  • Three choices at first, four later. One is always the right answer. The others are tiles from the pattern in the wrong place, plus one that doesn't belong at all.
  • Levels: AB (shown) → AAB → ABC → two things change at once (shape and color on separate beats) → growing rows (●, ●●, ●●●). Up a level after 3 first-try answers in a row, down one after 2 misses in a row.
  • Help: says a hint and removes one wrong choice. A second press removes another, leaving only the right one.
  • A wrong answer never ends the puzzle: the choice fades and the child tries again, so every puzzle ends on a success.

Open questions

  • Should each tile also say its name as the row is read, with a little bounce, so the rhythm is easier to hear?
  • Animals or toys instead of shapes for the easiest level?
