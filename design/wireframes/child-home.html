Says

On appear: “What do you want to practice? Reading, writing, spelling, math, puzzles, or a bit of everything.”

On a tile: its name (“Math!”) as the session starts.

Behavior

  • Six big tiles (about 400 × 290 pt), picture first, name second, so a non-reader can pick by picture.
  • Reading: the child reads aloud; on-device speech-to-text approves it, a grown-up can override.
  • Writing: letters, then whole words, in handwriting boxes.
  • Spelling: blanks in a word, growing until the whole word is written.
  • Math: flashcards, answer written in digit boxes. Puzzles: multiple choice.
  • A bit of everything: a mixed session drawn from all subjects with cards due.
  • Rewards (bottom, yellow): opens the claw machine and shows how many tokens there are.
  • ⚙️ Settings (top right) needs a press and hold (about 2 s); a plain tap says “Ask a grown-up.”

Open questions

  • Hide subjects with nothing due, or show them with “all done” stars?
  • Should a subject session be shorter than an “everything” session?
