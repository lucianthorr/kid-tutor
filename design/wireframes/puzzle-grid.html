Says

On appear: “Look at each row. Which one fills the gap?”

Tap a choice: that's the answer. There is no Check button: every puzzle has one answer, so the right or wrong reply comes straight away, and it names the tile.

Right: “Yes! This row is all triangles, and this column is all yellow. A yellow triangle!”

Wrong: “That's a triangle, good! But look down this column: what color are they?” (says what was right, then points at what was missed.)

Help, first press: the gap’s row glows. “This row is all triangles.” Second press: its column glows. “This column is all yellow.” Each press takes away a wrong choice.

Behavior

  • The puzzle on the left, choices on the right. Tapping a choice answers; no Check button.
  • The wrong choices are each right in one way and wrong in another (blue triangle: right shape, wrong color; yellow square: right color, wrong shape), plus one that's wrong in every way. A wrong answer tells the app which rule the child missed, and that goes in the log.
  • Levels: 2×2 where the bottom row repeats the top → 2×2 with a shape per row and a color per column → 3×3 (shown) → 3×3 where the count grows along each row (1, 2, 3) → the gap anywhere.
  • A wrong pick shows why: the line it doesn't fit (the gap's row or column) lights up in orange, as the app says to look there.
  • The hardest kind: it joins sessions once “what comes next?” reaches level 3.
