Says

“Spell friend. My friend came over to play. Friend.”

Behavior

  • The word is only spoken, never printed: printing it would give the answer away.
  • Each yellow box is its own handwriting box; the app reads it after the pencil pauses.
  • Check also happens automatically when every blank is filled.
  • Wrong letter: the box turns red and clears, “Almost! Try that letter again.” Second miss: the letter faded to trace.
  • Each word has its own level: level n means n random letters are missing. Two first tries in a row at a level move it up; a retry resets that count; needing a trace moves it down.
  • Top level (every letter missing): one wide box for the whole word, checked like Writing’s “look, cover, write”. Two first tries in a row there → mastered.
