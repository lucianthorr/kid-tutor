Says

On appear: “Which one is a ladder? Tap a picture to hear about it.”

Tap a picture: “Something with steps you climb to reach high places.”

Behavior

  • First tap selects a picture and reads its definition; “This one!” confirms.
  • Wrong choices come from words the child already knows well.
  • Wrong answer: that card fades and says what it actually is; child tries again.
  • Pictures: parent's own photos first, ARASAAC pictograms otherwise.

Open questions

  • Four choices or three for younger kids?
  • Show the word in text at all?
