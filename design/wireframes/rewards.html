Says

On appear: “You have 3 tokens. Move the claw over a toy, then press Drop.”

No tokens: “You need a token to play. Finish some practice to earn one!”

Win: “You got the bunny! It's going in your toy chest.”

Slip: “Ooh, it slipped out! Try grabbing a toy right in the middle. The claw will grab better next time.”

Nothing under the claw: “Oops, nothing there. The claw will grab better next time.”

Behavior

  • Reached from a Rewards button at the bottom of Home.
  • Move the claw by dragging the claw itself (touches elsewhere in the machine do nothing), or by holding the big arrows. Drop is the one main action; it costs one token.
  • The claw lowers, closes and lifts. If it caught a toy it carries it over a low glass wall and drops it into the open treasure chest on the left; then the toy appears big in the middle of the screen.
  • A full machine: 20 squishy toy balls in a messy pile: each tumbled in, rolled off what it landed on and came to rest against toys or the walls. They squash when they land and squeeze when the claw closes. When a toy is won, the ones leaning on it fall into the gap and a new one tumbles in.
  • Aim, not luck: the claw lands on the highest toy under it. Grabbed near its middle, the toy holds; grabbed off-center, it rides up and squeezes out, sooner the further off the claw was. No hidden randomness, and no highlight: lining up is the skill.
  • Misses help: each miss or slip makes the grip better, and the claw visibly opens wider. By the third try in a row, any drop onto the pile wins.
  • No duplicates: the machine shows toys not yet won, and refills once all of them have been won.
  • Tokens: 3 for every finished practice (grown-ups can change this). They never expire and are never taken away.

Later

  • Special plushes for milestones, and real-world reward tickets.
  • Photos of the child's own stuffed animals as prizes.
