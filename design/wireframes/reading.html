Says

“Read this word. Tap the microphone, then read it out loud.” (Never the word itself.)

Right: “Yes! Look.” · Miss: “Let's try once more.” · Second miss: “This word is look. Say it with me.”

Behavior

  • On-device speech recognition only; nothing is sent anywhere. Accepts the moment it hears the word.
  • Lenient: sound-alikes (to/two/too, see/sea) and one-letter slips in longer words count.
  • Waits as long as the child needs to start reading (no time limit while nothing is heard; tap the mic to stop). Once they start, it stops after a short silence or 7 seconds.
  • Tapping the word (or Help) reads it aloud and counts as help.
  • Grown-up buttons approve or skip by hand. If the iPad can't recognize speech on-device, they're the only way to answer.
  • Words first (sight words), short sentences once words are mastered.

Open questions

  • Show “I heard …” to the child, or only to grown-ups?
